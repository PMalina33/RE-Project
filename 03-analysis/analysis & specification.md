# Week 3 - Requirements Analysis and Specification
## 1. Information from Week 2
- The system should prevent two people from booking the same equipment at the same time.
- Equipment manager should be able to mark equipment as unavailable.
- Users should receive booking confirmations and reminders.
- The system should notify users about confirmation or rejection of booking.
## 2. Candidate Requirements
FIND: 
- The system should prevent two people from booking the same equipment at the same time.

DIAGNOSE:
- What is meant by prevent?
- Who is envolved?
- How would the system know?

ASK:
- Which people should be prevented from booking the same equipment?
- Who would get priority of booking?
- What if both users book simultaneously?
  
REPAIR:
- The system should not show an equipment when the equipment has already been reserved.
  
CHECK:
- Book an equipment on one user account and try booking the same equipment from a different user account.



FIND:
- Equipment manager should be able to mark equipment as unavailable.
  
DIAGNOSE:
- What is meant by equipment?
- Who is envolved?
  
ASK:
- Does the Equipment manager have control over all equipment?
- Is there anyone else who would be able to mark equipment too?
- Who would mark equipment available again?
  
REPAIR:
- An Equipment manager can track and control equipment availability.
  
CHECK:
- Let an Equipment manager track and control an equipments' status.


  
FIND:
- Users should receive booking confirmations and reminders.
  
DIAGNOSE:
- Who is envloved?
- What is meant by reminders?
  
ASK:
- What form of confirmation and reminders should be implemented?
- Should users have an account linked to get confirmations and reminders?
- Should reminders be for retuning of equipment only?
  
REPAIR:
- Booking confirmations and return reminders would be sent to a users account when an equipment has been reserved.

CHECK:
- Reserve/book an equipment and wait for confirmations and return reminders.



FIND:
- The system should notify users about confirmation or rejection of booking.
  
DIAGNOSE:
- Who is envolved?
- What is meant by rejection?
- How would that affect the system?
  
ASK:
- How should the users be notified?
- What impact would a rejection of a booking have?
- What if a user had a change of mind?

REPAIR:
- The system should send reminders electronically to staff and students about return due dates and confirmation of bookings.

CHECK:
- book an equiptment and check for reminders sent to the login source.
## 5. Quality Requirements
<u>Quality requirement: Secure password protection logins with double authentication.

Why does it matter?: To ensure users can track their bookings and no other users can login to oneanothers account and change or cancel other users bookings. Ensuring security.

How could it be checked?: Try to login under someone else account and password, wait for a authentication message.
