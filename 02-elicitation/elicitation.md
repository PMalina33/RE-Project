# Week 2 - Elicitation
## 1. Stakeholders
- Student
- Lecturer
- Equipment technician
- Department administrator
- Equipment manager
## 2. Stakeholder needs and concerns
- Student:
Needs: Knowing whether equipment is available.
Concerns: Being unable to get equipment when needed.
- Lecturer:
Needs: Have equipment available for teaching.
Concerns: Equipment not being available at the required time.
- Technician:
Needs: Knowing what equipment is due out and back.
Concerns: Late or missing equipment.
- Equipment manager:
Needs: Maintaining accountability for equipment.
Concerns: Equipment being lost or used without authorisation.
- Department administrator:
Needs: Tracking equipment bookings and what is used.
Concerns: Equipment being double booked or damaged.
## 3. Unknowns
- Who is allowed to book specialist equipment? : ask Equipment manager.
- What happens when equipment is returned late? : ask Technician.
- What equipment should be prioritised? : ask Lecturer.
- What information needs to be recorded about a booking? : ask Department administrator.
- How long is needed for an equipment to be returned? : ask Student.
## 4. Information sources
- Interviewing and asking questions to possible stakeholders.
## 5. Elicitation questions
- How can double bookings of an equipment be avoided?
- What should be done if an equipment cannot be returned by deadline?
- Who would be in charge of updating system records and tracking?
- What action can be taken if damaged equipment is returned?
## 6. Interview notes
STAKEHOLDER OUTCOMES LEARNED:
- The system should prevent two people from booking the same equipment at the same time.
- Equipment manager should be able to mark equipment as unavailable.
- Users should receive booking confirmations and reminders.
Still unknown: What range of equipments should be available.
## 7. Candidate requirements
- The system should notify users about confirmation or rejection of booking.
